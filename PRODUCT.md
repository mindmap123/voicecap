# Next Level Voice Agents Landing

## Product

Next Level sells custom digital systems for TPE/PME in France. This landing page promotes a voice agent offer focused on missed inbound calls, appointment booking, lead qualification, and automatic follow-up.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- Local service businesses, artisans, healthcare-adjacent practices, real estate agencies, training/coaching businesses, and small teams that receive valuable phone inquiries.
- Owners who are already busy, miss calls during work hours, and know that slow follow-up loses revenue.
- Non-technical buyers who need a clear business outcome, not AI vocabulary.

## Positioning

The market is crowded with generic "AI receptionist 24/7" promises. Next Level should position the offer as a measurable business system:

"We install a voice agent that answers, qualifies, books, and sends every lead into your tracking system."

The difference is not the voice model alone. The difference is the complete lead path:

1. Answer the call.
2. Understand the request.
3. Ask the right qualification questions.
4. Book or route the lead.
5. Send a structured summary.
6. Track outcomes so the business owner can see the value.

## Business Goal

Generate qualified leads for Next Level through a landing page with one primary action: request a missed-call audit.

Secondary action: listen to or request a live demo call.

## Tone

Direct, concrete, commercially sharp, and practical. Avoid AI hype. Avoid vague words like "innovative", "revolutionary", "seamless", and "optimize" unless tied to a concrete result.

## Offer Shape

Core offer:

- Voice agent setup for inbound calls.
- Scenario design by sector.
- Calendar or form handoff.
- Lead summary sent to email, Notion, Airtable, Google Sheets, or CRM.
- Basic dashboard and conversion tracking.
- Compliance-conscious call opening and RGPD mention.

Suggested packaging:

- Audit first: calculate missed-call cost.
- Pilot: one scenario, one number, one calendar or tracking destination.
- System: multiple scenarios, routing, lead tracking, reporting.
